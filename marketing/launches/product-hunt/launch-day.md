## Comment handling

Same rules as HN (see `../hacker-news/launch-day.md`), with PH tuning:

- Warmer register: PH rewards enthusiasm and story more than HN does. It still punishes
  press-release voice.
- Every commenter gets a reply, even "congrats on the launch" gets a thank-you.
- "How is this different from AppMockUp / Screenshots Pro / AppLaunchpad?" comes up on
  PH more than HN. Answer: those are no-code editors and SaaS; this is code-first, free,
  local, in your git repo, and your AI agent can drive it. Gracious, never dismissive.

## Do not

- Ask for upvotes anywhere, including DMs and group chats. PH detects vote networks and
  it is against the rules. "We're live today" with a link is fine.
- Launch and disappear: a dead maker comment section kills ranking velocity.
- Argue with a critical review.
